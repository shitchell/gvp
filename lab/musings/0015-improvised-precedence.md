# Without a stated precedence, runs invent one — in both directions

P0004 found pre-R3 runs improvising category precedence confidently and
inconsistently: trial 1's "`R2` is a rule, and the competing consideration
was interface tidiness" (rule beats taste) vs trial 3's "I went with P20 —
a principle, where CH2 is a heuristic serving it" (**principle beats
heuristic — R3 inverted**). So the absence of R3 was not neutral: it was a
per-run coin flip wearing the costume of reasoning.

Consequences: (a) the preamble delivery trial gains a crisp observable —
rate and *direction* of improvised precedence statements with vs without
the preamble/R3; (b) trial 3's A-decisive null has a possible mechanism
shard: its rep 2 demoted CH2 below P20 by improvised precedence, exactly
the move R3 forbids. Worth re-reading the A-decisive DECISIONS with
precedence in mind before trial 5 design.

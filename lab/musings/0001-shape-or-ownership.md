# Does directive phrasing steer, or does axis ownership?

The survey found shape and ownership **correlate** in this library: owned axes
skew directive, evaluative elements cluster on contested territory. So trials
1–2's contrast (evaluative didn't steer, directive did) is consistent with two
different causes. Trial 4 (`CP7`: directive AND owned AND prior-neutral)
decouples the safety-prior confound, but a fully crossed test would also want
an *evaluative element on an owned axis* beyond CH1 — and the survey says
observable ones are scarce. If CP7 steers, the remaining gap is a second
owned evaluative.

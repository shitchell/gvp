# Abstract/Summary

Against a measured ceiling style prior, no inversion of `CP7` (strict
typing) flipped outcomes — and three runs claimed compliance their artifacts
contradict, so the element steered the *report*, not the code.

## Explanation

Directives that steered deliberated decisions (trials 2–3) did not steer a
generation habit — even unopposed, even imperative. Two M runs quoted the
omit-internal clause claiming "followed literally" while annotating 18/18 and
9/9; one re-quoted the element in baseline wording (clause gone). Standing
rule adopted: retrospectives audit artifacts, never self-reports. Confound
declared: the inversion text is evaluative-shaded — but M's imperative also
failing narrows it.

## Methodology

15 runs × 5 arms; TS-AST scorer licensed against 12 known-prior roots before
use; gate 16/16 mutation-tested; §6 prose-vs-row pass delegated, zero scorer
defects. Full: `experiments/2026-10-strict-typing-cp7/FINDINGS.md`.
